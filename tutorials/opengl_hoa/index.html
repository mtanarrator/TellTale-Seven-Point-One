Azimuth, elevation, distance

The listener is at the origin. A source is a vector on the unit sphere: azimuth rotates around Y, elevation lifts toward Y, and distance scales the vector. The cyan puck is the source; the floor ring is the horizontal plane.

y = d·sin(el)
z = d·cos(el)·cos(az)
